# Billing and Refunds Policy

## Double Charges
In the event a customer is charged twice for the same billing cycle (Double Charge), support agents must immediately issue a full refund for the duplicate charge. The refund will take 3-5 business days to appear on the customer's credit card statement. No questions asked.

## Cancellations
Customers can cancel their subscription at any time. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing cycle. We do not provide prorated refunds for mid-cycle cancellations.
