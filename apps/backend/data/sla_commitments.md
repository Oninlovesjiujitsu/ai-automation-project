# Service Level Agreement (SLA) Commitments

## Response Times
- **Enterprise VIP Tier**: 1-hour response time, 24/7.
- **Pro Tier**: 12-hour response time, Business hours only (9 AM - 5 PM EST, Mon-Fri).
- **Basic/Free Tier**: 48-hour response time, best effort.

## SLA Breaches (Missed SLA)
If an SLA is breached for an Enterprise VIP customer, they are automatically entitled to a 5% credit on their next monthly invoice. Support agents should proactively offer this credit when resolving a late ticket.
