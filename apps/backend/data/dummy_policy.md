# SaaS Billing and Refund Policy

## Refunds
We offer a strict 14-day money-back guarantee for all new subscriptions. 
If a user requests a refund after 14 days, the refund must be denied, but we can offer a 10% discount on their next month.

## Account Cancellation
Users can cancel their account at any time through the billing dashboard. We do not require a phone call to cancel.

## Downgrades
Downgrading a plan will take effect at the end of the current billing cycle. Prorated refunds are not issued for mid-cycle downgrades.
